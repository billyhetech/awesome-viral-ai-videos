## Prompt

```text
Create a 30-second, 1080p ultra-realistic personal home video of a young Korean woman running a small summer errand in an old Seoul residential neighborhood. It should feel like an unplanned recording by a friend using an early-2000s DV camcorder.

SUBJECT: A naturally pretty Korean woman in her early 20s, realistic skin texture, minimal makeup, long black hair in a loose low ponytail with wispy strands. She wears a white sleeveless knit top, faded-blue denim shorts, white ankle socks, black canvas sneakers, and a thin silver bracelet. Maintain identical face, hairstyle, outfit, and proportions throughout.

SETTING & STYLE: Quiet, lived-in Seoul streets with narrow concrete lanes, modest houses, weathered walls, potted flowers, parked bicycles, overhead wires, hanging laundry, and a tiny local shop. Warm summer afternoon, ordinary neighborhood life, no recognizable brands or tourist attractions.

Use shaky handheld footage, imperfect framing, occasional autofocus hunting, exposure shifts, faded colors, soft digital detail, mild noise, motion blur, and subtle camcorder handling sounds. No stabilization, cinematic camera moves, dramatic lighting, or commercial polish.

30-SECOND SEQUENCE:

- 0–5s: She exits her house with a cloth tote, checks the door, notices the camera, smiles, and says, “I'm just going to the shop.”
- 5–11s: She greets an elderly neighbor with a polite bow, then steps aside as a little girl rides past on a bicycle, its bell ringing. The camera briefly follows the bicycle.
- 11–18s: Wind shakes a flowering pot, scattering small flowers. She picks one up, gently returns it to the planter's edge, smiles at the camera, and continues.
- 18–25s: At a tiny neighborhood shop, she takes a reusable container from her tote, receives a small paper-wrapped item from the shopkeeper, thanks them, and puts it away.
- 25–30s: She walks back past the same houses and bicycles. Her ponytail moves in the breeze as she waves playfully at the camera, reaches her doorway, turns back with a smile, and the footage abruptly cuts to black.

AUDIO: Natural location sound only—footsteps, bicycle bell, distant scooters, birds, summer insects, rustling leaves, faint neighborhood chatter, shop door, and subtle camcorder noise. No music, narration, added sound effects, or other dialogue.

IMPORTANT: Prioritize authentic everyday behavior, natural expressions, realistic timing, consistent identity, and the imperfect charm of an old personal home video. Avoid staged acting, exaggerated gestures, artificial skin, visual glitches, or an AI-generated appearance.
```
