## Prompt

```text
Video specifications:
16:9 horizontal video, 30 seconds, realistic live-action cinematography, 24fps, outdoor daytime, with synchronized environmental sound and upbeat game-show music. No subtitles, no watermark, no logo, no on-screen graphics, no brand text.

Overall scene:
A sunny outdoor summer water-obstacle variety show. The location is a large clear turquoise swimming pool with realistic reflections and ripples. The course is built from pink, white, and pale-yellow inflatable and plastic obstacle elements. In the background there are packed spectator stands, white railings, colorful triangular flags, palm trees, blue sky, and soft white clouds. The mood is bright, refreshing, loud, playful, and energetic, similar to a Japanese or Korean TV water challenge show. Colors are vivid but not cartoonish. Materials must look real, with clear water splashes, wet hair, and glossy surfaces.

Main character:
A young Asian woman in her 20s with a natural athletic build, wearing a pink ruffled one-piece swimsuit. She has wet black hair, slightly messy from the water, natural makeup, and an expression that shifts between nervous, excited, and amused. Her movement should feel real: she hesitates, loses balance, fights to recover, and laughs at herself. She should not move like a perfect professional athlete. She remains the clear main subject throughout the entire video.

Shot-by-shot timeline:

0-3 seconds:
Low water-level wide-angle opening shot. The camera is half close to the pool surface, showing small turquoise ripples in the foreground and the obstacle course ahead. The main character stands on the starting platform in the distance, looking up at several hanging pink rings above her. Strong sunlight reflects on the water. The spectator stands are visible but slightly soft in the background. The camera slowly pushes forward.

3-7 seconds:
Medium close tracking shot as the main character runs from the white starting platform and jumps onto a giant glossy pink inflatable ball. When her foot lands, the ball sinks slightly and wobbles. She spreads both arms to keep balance, her face showing panic mixed with laughter. The camera has subtle handheld energy to emphasize instability and tension.

7-11 seconds:
Cut to a side low-angle shot. The main character stands unsteadily on the pink ball, with the pool reflecting the ball underneath. Her knees bend, toes grip, and her body sways left and right. The audience cheers in the background. At the end of this segment, she barely manages to jump toward the next round white platform.

11-16 seconds:
Overhead shot. A round white platform sits in the center of the pool. A pink-and-white striped rotating sweeper bar moves across the platform from left to right. The main character runs in small steps to avoid it. She jumps over the first pass, then ducks awkwardly under the second pass. The bar rotates at a moderate readable speed, not too fast and not blurred. Her ruffled swimsuit and wet hair move naturally with her motion.

16-20 seconds:
Front tracking shot. She runs across a narrow bridge with yellow railings while pink side devices spray water mist inward. Water hits her shoulders and the side of her face. She squints, laughs, loses her rhythm for a moment, then keeps running. The camera moves backward to keep her centered. Sunlight catches the mist as small sparkling droplets.

20-25 seconds:
Slow-motion side shot. She leaps from the edge of a white platform, body extended horizontally, arms reaching forward toward the next platform. The spectator stands, colorful flags, and blue sky are visible behind her. The pool glitters below. The action should feel exaggerated in a fun game-show way but still physically believable. Maintain slow motion for about 2 seconds, then return to normal speed.

25-30 seconds:
She fails to grab the platform edge and falls into the pool, creating a large realistic splash. Cut to a water-level close-up as she resurfaces, wet hair stuck to her face. She catches her breath and starts laughing. The crowd cheers behind her and ripples spread across the water. The final second holds on her smiling face near the platform, ending naturally.

Cinematography style:
Realistic TV variety-show filming style, bright natural sunlight, high shutter speed for crisp water splashes, slight wide-angle lens, dynamic tracking shots, clean and sharp image, lively but controlled editing. Dominant colors: turquoise water, pink obstacles, white platforms, yellow accents. Camera movement should feel energetic but not chaotic. Human anatomy, face, and body motion must remain stable and consistent.

Audio design:
Include audio for the full 30 seconds. Background music is upbeat, playful, energetic game-show music with a light sense of tension. Layer realistic location sound: crowd cheering and laughing, distant excited host-style shouting without clearly understandable words, wet footsteps on platforms, soft inflatable-object friction, water spray, and splashes. The splash sound should peak at 25-30 seconds when she falls into the pool. After she resurfaces, keep music and crowd cheers going, with subtle breathing and laughter from the main character.

Negative constraints:
No text, no subtitles, no brand marks, no corner TV logo, no UI frame, no cartoon style, no animation look, no plastic skin, no distorted hands or feet, no extra person stealing focus, no character identity change, no sudden costume change, no indoor scene, no night scene, no rain, no over-sexualization, no wardrobe malfunction, no injury, no blood, no horror mood, no excessive motion blur, no broken water physics, no impossible floating or flying.
```
