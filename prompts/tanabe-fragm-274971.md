## Prompt

```text
15s | 16:9 | 24fps | Olympic 100m final, night. One sprinter in lane 4 —
the tallest man in the field — starts level with the pack, then unfolds and
runs away from them, winning in 9.4 seconds roughly four meters clear.
Photoreal broadcast cinematography, heat-soaked stadium air, explosive crowd
energy.

=== GLOBAL SETUP ===

ENVIRONMENT & TEXTURE
Night. Olympic stadium, 80,000-seat bowl packed to the roof. Humid summer
air. Heat haze shimmering off the blue mondo track. Emphasize extremely
realistic physical texture: sweat beading and running, muscle fiber shifting
under skin, spike plates biting into the rubber, grit kicking up behind the
blocks.

VISUAL STYLE
Photoreal live sports broadcast. Shallow depth of field on close shots, deep
focus on wides. Hard key from stadium floodlights overhead. Strong rim light
separating the runners from the dark crowd. Thousands of camera flashes
popping across the stands as a moving field of white specks.

CAMERA LANGUAGE
Broadcast telephoto and trackside rail cameras. Exactly ONE camera move per
time slice. Never combine moves.

RUNNERS
Lane 4 (hero): 26-year-old African-American male sprinter, 192cm tall —
visibly the tallest man on the track, a clear head above the shortest runner
in the field. Deep brown skin with a wet sheen under the floodlights — keep
the real fine pores and natural skin texture. Long neck, broad flat
shoulders, very long legs, narrow hips, deep chest, long muscle bellies
rather than bulk. Sharp cheekbones, jaw clenched at the start, eyes locked
dead ahead on the finish line, never glancing sideways. Short cropped hair
with a close fade. Yellow-and-green singlet, bib number 4. Veins raised on
the forearms.
  Stride signature — this must be readable on screen: he takes FEWER strides
  than anyone else, about 41 to their 45. Each stride is visibly longer and
  covers more ground per contact. Because of his height he needs more
  distance to reach full upright posture, so he is NOT ahead early.
Chasing pack: seven other sprinters, treated as ONE group. Never featured
individually, never in focus. Shorter, more compact builds. Higher stride
frequency, choppier. Mixed dark and light singlets.
Crowd: an undifferentiated mass, out of focus, rising to their feet in a wave.

PERFORMANCE CORE
The story is the delay and the release. He is level with the field for the
first third, then unfolds and eats the gap alive. Show dominance through
stride length and empty track, not through slow-motion tricks.

FORBIDDEN
No subtitles. No captions. No on-screen graphics, scoreboard, timer, or any
legible text or numbers. No commentator voice. No dialogue of any kind. No
background music. No runners appearing or vanishing — exactly eight on the
track for the entire 15 seconds. Hero's face, hair, height, and singlet must
not change between shots. No flickering limbs, no duplicated arms or legs,
no warped feet.

=== TIMESTAMPS ===

0-2s / Frame 0-48 — THE STILLNESS
Action: Eight sprinters in the set position, hips high, fingertips on the
  line. Hero in lane 4, center frame — even folded into the blocks he is
  obviously the longest body in the row, knees stacked higher than anyone
  else's. A single bead of sweat crawls down his temple and hangs at his jaw.
  Absolute stillness. Crowd noise drops to a low hum.
Intent: Compress the energy. The stiller this is, the more violent the start
  reads.
Camera: Low-angle trackside rail, side-on. LOCKED OFF, no movement.
Sound: Low crowd hum, one distant whistle, the hero's held breath.
Negative: no subtitles, no music.

Frame 48 (2.0s) — THE GUN
The starter's pistol cracks. The report slaps off the stadium bowl and comes
back as an echo.

2-5s — LEVEL (0-30m)
Action: All eight fire out of the blocks. Footplates kick backward. Spikes
  tear at the track. The seven shorter runners snap upright fast, legs
  churning at high frequency. The hero stays low and long, still folded
  forward, unwinding one huge stride at a time. Through 30 meters he is dead
  level with the field — for a beat he even looks a fraction behind.
Intent: Hold the audience. Make them doubt him.
Camera: Side tracking rail matching race speed. ONE lateral move, left to
  right.
Sound: Crowd roar detonating, spikes ripping rubber, explosive breathing.
Negative: no subtitles, no music, no commentator.

5-8s — THE UNFOLDING (30-60m)
Action: He finally comes to full upright posture, later than everyone else —
  and the stride opens to its full length. Each contact now swallows the
  ground the others need two steps for. At 45 meters he draws level and goes
  past. Sweat flies off his shoulders in visible droplets, catching the
  floodlights. By 60 meters he is two body lengths clear.
Intent: The moment the race stops being a race.
Camera: Long telephoto, head-on from the finish line. ONE slow push in.
  Compressed perspective stacks the pack behind him.
Sound: Sustained roar building, a rolling wall of sound.
Negative: no subtitles, no music.

8-11.4s — ALONE (60-100m)
Action: His face settles into the top-end sprint mask — jaw loose, cheeks
  vibrating with each footstrike. Enormous, unhurried strides. Behind him the
  pack is contorted, straining, taking far more steps to cover the same
  ground. Clean empty track between him and the nearest rival, about four
  meters. Heat haze distorting the air in that gap.
Intent: Let the empty track do the work.
Camera: Low tracking rail alongside the hero. ONE steady lateral move.
Sound: Crowd at a continuous peak, breathing, spikes.
Negative: no subtitles, no music.

Frame 274 (11.4s) — THE LINE
Action: Chest hits the line. Torso dips hard. Long arms flung back. The
  timing beam breaks.
Camera: Side-on at the finish. Drop to slow motion for a beat, then return
  to real time.
Sound: The roar cresting into a single detonation.

11.4-13s — THE REST ARRIVE
Action: The chasing pack crosses roughly four-tenths of a second later,
  strung out, gasping, hands on heads. The hero is already past them in the
  frame, decelerating, towering over them as they slow around him.
Camera: Wide, ONE slow pull back to reveal the full straight.
Sound: Roar, then a ragged edge of disbelief in it.

13-15s — ECSTASY
Action: He wheels around, both long arms flung wide, mouth open in a roar
  that is swallowed by the crowd. Camera flashes storm across the stands in
  waves. He drops to his knees on the track, head tipped back, chest heaving.
Intent: Release everything the first two seconds held down.
Camera: Handheld, tight, moving with him. ONE move, following.
Sound: Crowd at maximum. A solid wall of sound with no gaps in it.
Negative: no subtitles, no music, no commentator voice.

=== CLOSING ===
Throughout all 15 seconds: night stadium under floodlights, humid heat haze,
telephoto broadcast look. Hero in lane 4 always weighted toward frame center,
always the tallest figure on the track, always taking longer strides than
anyone around him. Keep his face, hair, build, and yellow-and-green singlet
identical in every shot. Keep exactly eight runners on the track the entire
time. No subtitles, no captions, no on-screen text or numbers, no commentator
voice, no dialogue, no background music. Sound is crowd, spikes, breathing,
and the starter's pistol only.
```
