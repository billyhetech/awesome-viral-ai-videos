```text
Cinematic photorealistic fantasy, 30 seconds, 16:9, warm golden sunbeams through tall gothic windows, floating candles, soft haze and dust in the light, rich film look, shallow depth of field, smooth gentle camera moves, no text, no watermark.

SETTING: A vast medieval magic-school dining hall with long dark wooden tables, stone walls, tall arched windows, hundreds of candles floating near the ceiling, hanging house banners (green with a silver serpent, red with a gold lion), students in black robes eating and talking.

SHOT 1 (0-2s): Wide shot of the hall. The blonde girl sits reading an open old book at a long table with a white teacup. On the right the two bully boys laugh and shove the small girl, who holds a stack of books and a sack and bends under the weight.

SHOT 6 (9-11s): She calmly sips tea from the white cup, wand in her other hand, while a sack floats above the tied boys and pours white flour over them.
SHOT 7 (11-13s): The boys, bound with rope and covered in white flour, sit stunned, a basket of eggs floating in the air above them.
SHOT 8 (13-15s): Close-up of one boy, wide-eyed, flour on his face and an egg yolk dripping down his head.

SHOT 11 (19-21s): A huge cloud of white flour smoke bursts around the boys, a sack hanging in the air above.
SHOT 12 (21-23s): Close-up: the flour-covered boy with a raw egg on top of his head stares forward, the blonde girl's raised finger blurred in the foreground.
SHOT 13 (23-25s): The blonde girl's hair and shoulder in the foreground; in focus the boy's flour-covered face, with the egg still on top of his head.

SHOT 16 (29-30s): Wide shot: the blonde girl stands up calmly and proudly facing the camera, the two bound floured boys behind her and the small girl smiling with her books and the green cloth. Hold on the sunbeams.

AUDIO: soft magical orchestral music, murmurs of students, rope whipping, wand whoosh, flour bursts and eggs splatting, the blonde girl's calm short words. No subtitles.
```
