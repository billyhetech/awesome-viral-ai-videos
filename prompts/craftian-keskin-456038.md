## Prompt

```text
Style: Hyper-realistic cinematic Western revenge film,  grounded gunfight choreography, realistic body mechanics, dramatic facial acting, dusty frontier atmosphere, sweeping cinematic camera, emotional storytelling, ultra-detailed costumes, practical effects, realistic physics.

Duration: 30 seconds

Aspect Ratio: 16:9

IMPORTANT

Keep the SAME characters exactly as the reference sheets.

— RED: long crimson coat, red hat covering his eyes, twin revolvers, calm, controlled, emotionally scarred.

— SKULLY: black suit, skull mask, black hat, twin revolvers, cold and merciless.

Everything must feel like a real Western film.

No exaggerated action.

Realistic gun handling.

Heavy emotional acting.

Fast cinematic editing during the flashback.

------------------------------------------------

[00:00–00:03]

Extreme close-up of RED's face beneath the brim of his hat. Only his mouth and clenched jaw are visible. Wind blows his red coat gently. Dust drifts across the empty main street.

Cut to an extreme close-up of SKULLY's skull mask. His fingers slowly tighten around his revolver grip. Silence except for wind and creaking wooden signs.

------------------------------------------------

[00:03–00:10]

Rapid flashback montage.

Fast multishots with quick cuts and bright white flash transitions.

— RED is violently pinned to the muddy ground by three ruthless outlaws.

— His hands claw desperately through the dirt.

— SKULLY drags RED's terrified wife toward a waiting horse.

— Close-up of her tear-filled face.

— She stretches her hand toward RED.

— She screams:

"REEEEED!!"

— RED screams back, struggling violently.

— Boots stomp him back into the dirt.

— Close-up of their fingertips almost touching but never reaching.

— SKULLY mounts the horse with her.

— Horse gallops away through clouds of dust.

— RED continues screaming helplessly as the flashback burns away into white.

------------------------------------------------

[00:10–00:17]

Back to the present.

Wide cinematic shot of the deserted Wild West main street.

Citizens quietly gather on wooden porches, balconies and outside the saloon.

Bartender freezes while cleaning a glass.

A child is pulled indoors.

The sheriff watches silently.

Dust devils roll between RED and SKULLY.

Their coats sway gently.

No one speaks.

------------------------------------------------

[00:17–00:21]

Alternating close-ups.

RED's hand hovers beside his revolver.

SKULLY smiles beneath the skull mask.

Close-up on his mouth.

He quietly says:

"You will meet your wife soon."

Silence.

Only wind.

A loose tumbleweed rolls between them.

Extreme close-up of both trigger fingers.

------------------------------------------------

[00:21–00:25]

The duel.

Ultra-fast multishots.

Boots planting.

Coats whipping.

Hands drawing revolvers.

Extreme close-up of spinning cylinders.

Simultaneous gunshots.

Massive muzzle flashes.

Echoes across the town.

Silence immediately follows.

One second passes.

SKULLY slowly drops to his knees.

His revolver slips from his hand.

He collapses face-first into the dusty street.

------------------------------------------------

[00:25–00:30]

Ground-level POV beside the fallen SKULLY.

His vision blurs.

RED slowly walks into frame.

His long crimson coat fills the screen.

He stops over SKULLY.

Camera tilts upward from the ground as RED points his revolver directly down at him.

Close-up of RED's expression.

Calm.

Cold.

Without emotion.

He quietly says:

"Not yet."

A final gunshot.

Cut instantly to black.

Audio

No music.

Only cinematic Western sound design:

constant desert wind, distant crows, creaking wooden buildings, leather movement, boots on dirt, horse snorts in the flashback, muffled crowd breathing, clothing rustle, revolver hammers cocking, realistic gunshots with long canyon echoes, shell casings hitting the ground, SKULLY collapsing into dust, and complete silence after the final shot.

Negative prompts

No slow motion except subtle emphasis during the flashback. No superhero movement. No unrealistic gunplay. No floating cameras. No modern objects. No blurry characters. No cartoon effects. No excessive blood. Prioritize emotional facial acting, cinematic framing, realistic pacing, and frequent shot changes with strong visual storytelling.
```
