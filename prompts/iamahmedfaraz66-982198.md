## Prompt

```text
Create a 15-second ultra-realistic early-2000s Sony MiniDV home video in 16:9 landscape format.

The same 24-year-old Korean woman with long, loose dark hair, minimal makeup, and realistic skin wears her familiar oversized maroon hoodie, loose casual pants, and canvas shoulder bag. Maintain her exact facial features, hairstyle, outfit, and body proportions throughout.

Shot 1 (0–3 seconds): A medium-wide handheld shot inside a cozy little neighborhood toy shop. She stands beside a wooden display counter and notices a tiny colorful wind-up toy car. Her eyes brighten with curiosity. She picks it up, examines its little metal winding key, and smiles as if she has discovered something unexpectedly fun.

Shot 2 (3–6 seconds): Cut to a close-up of her hands placing the toy car on the wooden counter. She carefully winds the key several times, then positions the car at the edge of a clear stretch of tabletop. Her fingers hover behind it in anticipation.

Shot 3 (6–9 seconds): Cut to a low-angle close-up at tabletop level. She releases the car, and it suddenly rattles forward, moving faster than she expected. It veers slightly to one side. The camera follows its little journey imperfectly, capturing its tiny wheels spinning and its body wobbling realistically.

Shot 4 (9–12 seconds): Cut to a medium shot of her leaning over the counter, following the toy with exaggerated concentration. When it turns unexpectedly toward the edge, she quickly reaches out and gently stops it with one finger before it falls. She looks relieved, then bursts into a delighted giggle.

Shot 5 (12–15 seconds): Cut to a closer shot of her face and the toy. She carefully sets it back at the starting point, looks toward the camera with a mischievous grin, and gestures as if inviting the person filming to watch another race. She winds it again as the clip ends.

Visual style: Authentic early-2000s Sony MiniDV home-video aesthetic, filmed by another person standing nearby. Subtle handheld shake, imperfect framing, natural camera repositioning, occasional autofocus hunting, mild exposure shifts, faded colors, soft contrast, and realistic consumer-camcorder texture. Use distinct cuts between all five shots, keeping the toy, shop counter, lighting, and character appearance consistent. The toy should look like a simple inexpensive wind-up toy, not a modern remote-controlled car.

Audio: Natural toy-shop ambience, the tiny metallic winding clicks, the toy's light rattling against the wooden counter, faint distant shop sounds, and her genuine quiet laughter. No background music, narration, or artificial sound effects.

Motion quality: Smooth, continuous real-time movement at a consistent frame rate. The toy must move with believable mechanical motion, realistic wheel rotation, and natural friction. Her hand movements and reactions should be fluid and spontaneous. No stuttering, judder, frame skipping, duplicated frames, slow motion, excessive motion blur, or unnatural object movement.

The final video should feel like a friend capturing a spontaneous moment of childlike excitement in an ordinary little toy shop—playful, warm, charming, and genuinely nostalgic.
```
