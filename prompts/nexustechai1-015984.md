20-second emotional Korean family drama. A Korean daughter discovers her father asleep over his desk and briefly believes he has died. Her calls to him carry the emotional hook. All dialogue is spoken in native Korean.

CHARACTERS AND LOCATION
Father: Korean man in his late 60s, thinning gray hair, charcoal blazer and dark open-collar shirt.
Daughter: Korean woman in her late 20s, dark ponytail with soft bangs and a black blouse.
Warm wooden office, desk with folders and leather writing pad, green leather chair, bookshelves and miniature buses. Soft afternoon daylight through blinds.

CAMERA
Handheld throughout: gentle visible hand tremor, breathing sway and small framing corrections. The operator physically steps closer for push-ins and follows movement with slight human delay. Maintain readable faces and consistent eyelines.

She touches his shoulder and searches his face:
“아빠…?”
Her voice is small and breathy, rising slightly with uncertainty. She waits for him to move. He remains asleep. Her fingers tighten against his jacket.

00:02–00:05 — SECOND CALL
HARD CUT to her close-up from his seated eye level. She leans closer, looking directly at him below the lens.
Her breath catches. Her eyebrows draw together, her lower lip trembles and her eyes fill.
She shakes his shoulder and calls:
“아빠!”
The word begins urgently but breaks midway into a pleading sob. She is trying to reach him, not simply shouting louder. Her mouth remains slightly open afterward as she waits, terrified, for an answer.
The operator takes a small, urgent step closer. Hold her reaction.

FATHER, softly and tenderly:
“아빠 죽은 줄 알았어?”

00:11–00:15 — HER PLEA
Stay on her face. She draws an uneven breath and looks into his eyes.

“아빠… 평생 내 곁에 있어 줘.”

He wraps both arms firmly around her and cradles the back of her head, resting his cheek against her hair.
The handheld camera edges closer and settles on her tearful face against his shoulder. End while he is still holding her.

The daughter’s first call asks for a response; the second begs him to come back. Keep her attention entirely on his face. Build fear through listening, interrupted breath and increasingly urgent touch. Her crying becomes stronger only after he wakes and she feels safe.

The father listens carefully and responds to her fear. His question carries tenderness, and his embrace is immediate and protective. Natural blinking, believable pauses and emotionally responsive eyes.

Clear Korean dialogue, breath catching, quiet sobs, chair movement and clothing rustle. Keep her two calls intimate and prominent in the sound mix. Dialogue and SFX only; no music.
