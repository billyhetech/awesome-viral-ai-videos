Create a 30-second ultra-realistic Korean home video from around 2003, casually recorded by a friend using an early-2000s consumer MiniDV camcorder. Capture an ordinary, slightly lazy evening with spontaneous moments, imperfect framing, and authentic everyday behavior.

**CHARACTER:** A young adult Korean woman in her early 20s with natural beauty, realistic skin texture, minimal makeup, and long black hair loosely tied in a messy side ponytail.
