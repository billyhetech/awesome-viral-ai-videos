## Prompt

```text
a completely new 30-second viral-looking raw roadside smartphone video. 16:9, one continuous unbroken handheld take from a passenger inside a nearby moving car. Realistic daylight highway, natural road shake, autofocus hunting, dirty windshield reflections, wind noise, traffic sounds, exposure shifts, compression, and awkward digital zoom. No cinematic polish.

A weathered American pickup drives beside a civilian sedan. In the open truck bed: a small inflatable pool, portable BBQ, cooler, and a father cooking while an adult woman relaxes in the pool.

0–5s: Father casually tosses a grilled hot dog toward the sedan passenger, who catches it. Camera zooms in and struggles to focus.

5–10s: A golden retriever jumps into the truck bed and grabs the hot dog through the gap between vehicles. Everyone reacts in disbelief.

10–16s: Dog runs around the bed, knocking over the cooler and utensils while water splashes from the pool. Woman screams, then laughs. Father tries to catch the dog.

16–22s: Cars slow. Dog stands proudly on the tailgate with the hot dog. Camera rapidly zooms, overshoots, loses focus, then refocuses.

22–27s: Father approaches slowly. Dog suddenly jumps into the pool, creating a huge splash.

27–30s: Wet dog sits proudly in the pool holding the stolen hot dog while everyone laughs. Traffic continues behind them. End naturally with shaky phone footage.

Everything must feel physically believable and causally connected. No cuts, slow motion, music, subtitles, captions, logos, watermarks, text, or brand names.
```
