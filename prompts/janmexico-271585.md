## Prompt

```text
Jan: tall slim, round red glasses, earpiece right ear, gloves, harness.
Tone: IMAX anamorphic 2x, golden hour. Hard low sun on wet concrete, deep blue shadows, backlight, horizontal blue flares, saturated epic grade. Never grey or flat.
CAMERA: low angle nearly every beat. Crash zooms, straight tracking, tilts. NEVER orbit or arc. Smooth motion, natural blur, 24fps, no stop motion. Jan never looks into the lens.
AUDIO: Renteria only by radio, thin, tinny, filtered, click each side. Jan answers out loud. SFX only: wind, rotor, rope, fabric. NO MUSIC, no subtitles.
0-6s Ground-level low angle, sun flare on puddles. Crash zoom to Jan's hands snapping the case shut, tilt up to earpiece and face, front to camera. Renteria, urgent: "Treinta segundos, Jan. Cincuenta referencias. Una sola toma. Tienes que mandarlo ya."
6-9s Low angle into the sun, camera tracks back ahead of Jan. He lifts the case: "Enterado. Voy por el." and runs.
9-12s Water-level low angle. Jan sprints over the camera, jumps a duct, gold spray on the lens. Renteria: "Cuando te subas, abres tu Capcot y lo generas ya."
12-15s Low angle on Duarte, rim lit. Crash zoom: "Pense que llegabas mas tarde."
15-18s Low angle, helicopter drops into a low hover, rotor wash blasting gold spray. Jan steps up through the door.
18-21s Cabin, over the shoulder behind Jan. He opens the case revealing the laptop, screen square to camera. Push in as he presses Generate.
21-25s Jan clips a rappel line to his harness, grips it with both gloved hands, kicks off the skid and swings out over the facade. Camera whips down with him into a fast controlled descent, boots striking the glass, sunlight tearing past.
25-30s Epic finale. Mid descent Jan yanks a second rope hard. The giant banner bursts open beside him and unrolls floor by floor, snapping taut. He lands on his feet in the plaza. Crash zoom out to a vast skyline wide, the full banner reading ESTO ES SEEDANCE 2.5 above him, helicopter rising into the sunburst.
```
