## Prompt

```text
Create a 15-second, 16:9 psychedelic horror music video: 30 distinct animated compositions, an average of 0.5 seconds per shot. Start at full intensity on the first frame. Make the supplied artworks come violently alive inside a corrupted, hypnotic nightmare.

REFERENCE DIRECTION
Use all supplied images as visual references. Preserve their recognizable silhouettes, intricate ornament, chrome surfaces, skulls, horns, jeweled details, printed textures, deep blacks, blood-red accents and electric rainbow colors. Develop additional compositions from these motifs to reach 30 shots. Preserve the mix of photographic detail and graphic illustration. Each shot fills the screen.
MOTION PRIORITY
The subjects themselves must move, not just the camera. Use one dominant, instantly readable action per shot:

Horned figures snap their heads toward the viewer; metallic blindfolds ripple and ribbons whip outward.
Chrome mouths open abruptly; keys twist, chains swing and skull-shaped jewelry vibrates.

Uncanny deer jerk forward; antlers branch rapidly and nearby plants coil inward.
Skeletal warriors slash across the frame; armor plates shift and radial patterns spin behind them.
A psychedelic mechanical tunnel contracts and pulls the camera through its center.
Giant cybernetic skulls loom over a neon city; jaw mechanisms snap and buildings bend around them.
Chrome bird-skull creatures thrust their beaks toward the lens; segmented necks coil and unfold.
A skeletal astronaut snaps toward camera as neon vapor pours through the helmet.
Striped cakes twist like living sculptures; cream stretches, cherries levitate and red syrup flows upward.
Horned skull idols rotate abruptly; ornaments orbit their faces and printed layers slip out of registration.
An ornate oiran-like figure turns sharply; hair ornaments vibrate, sleeves whip and metallic ribs expand.
A jeweled black cat fixes its gaze on the viewer, then lunges toward the lens as its ornaments scatter into light.
Use different actions and framing when a motif returns. Make every scene visibly animate from its first frame. Keep the main subject recognizable beneath the distortion.
EDITING / 15-SECOND STRUCTURE
0.0-3.0s: 5 shots, approximately 0.6s each. Open with an extreme close-up of a horned face snapping toward camera on the first kick. Cut through a metallic mouth, a deer, a skeletal warrior and a tunnel. Immediate impact; no intro.
3.0-6.0s: 6 shots, alternating approximately 0.4s and 0.6s. Neon city skulls, chrome bird creatures, astronaut and cat. Hard cuts, whip-pan transitions and sudden changes of scale.
6.0-9.0s: 6 shots, alternating approximately 0.4s and 0.6s. Warping desserts, horned idols and the ornate figure. Surfaces stretch, liquefy and snap back. Push through an eye, mouth or spiral into the next composition.
9.0-12.0s: 6 shots, alternating approximately 0.4s and 0.6s. Return to earlier motifs with more aggressive actions, extreme close-ups, inverted viewpoints and brief kaleidoscopic multiplication.
12.0-15.0s: 7 shots: six approximately 0.4s shots followed by a 0.6s final shot. Maximum kinetic intensity. End with a horned skull rushing into the lens, the image tearing into chromatic fragments, then an abrupt cut to black on the final distorted impact. No fade.

GLITCH / NOISE
Dense, deliberate analog and digital corruption: RGB channel separation, horizontal scanline tears, VHS tracking errors, CRT interference, granular static, datamosh trails, pixel-sorting streaks, block displacement, color inversion, frame skipping and short motion echoes. Place strong glitches on cuts, kicks and snare fills. Use brief 1-3-frame disruptions and textured noise bursts while keeping the subjects visible. Distortion must react to moving forms. Alternate crisp, disturbing detail with explosive corruption. Avoid prolonged full-screen white flashes.

CAMERA
Aggressive crash zooms, rapid lateral whip pans, short barrel rolls, macro-to-wide jumps and forward dives through openings. Choose one main camera move per shot. Preserve clear focal points and deep layered motion.
AUDIO
Original instrumental EDM / dark psychedelic trance at 150 BPM. Start immediately with a heavy four-on-the-floor kick and tight rolling sixteenth-note bass. Add distorted acid arpeggios, unsettling resonant synths, metallic percussion, reverse swells and granular digital noise. Synchronize cuts, subject impacts and glitches to beats and offbeats. Increase density toward the final second. No vocals, speech or narration.
AVOID
Static slideshow frames, simple Ken Burns motion, slow motion, gentle crossfades, long establishing shots, cute cheerful mood, generic smooth plastic rendering, persistent split screens, contact-sheet layouts, added captions, titles, subtitles, logos and watermarks.
```
