## Prompt

```text
Visual Style
Ultra-photorealistic live-action Korean mystery drama, indistinguishable from footage captured on an ARRI Alexa 35 with anamorphic lenses. Premium Netflix-quality production. Natural performances, realistic human movement, consistent protagonist throughout every frame, physically accurate lighting, ray-traced reflections, realistic skin texture, authentic Seoul architecture, subtle cinematic color grading, shallow depth of field, realistic handheld stabilization, smooth Steadicam movement, premium production design, HDR, 4K. Absolutely no AI artifacts, morphing, flickering, identity drift, warped hands, unrealistic expressions, impossible transitions, or abrupt scene changes.
0:00–0:05 | Rainy Evening
A peaceful rainy evening in Seoul.
A slow cinematic drone shot reveals narrow streets glowing beneath warm storefront lights. Rain falls gently onto wet pavement, creating beautiful reflections of cafés, bookstores, and street lamps.
The camera slowly descends to street level.
A beautiful young Korean woman, around 24 years old, walks naturally along the sidewalk carrying a transparent umbrella. She wears a beige trench coat, cream knit sweater, black trousers, and white sneakers. Her long black hair moves gently with the breeze. Other pedestrians pass naturally without acknowledging her. Traffic flows realistically in the background.
Everything feels like a real Korean film.
0:05–0:10 | The Bookstore
She notices a quiet independent bookstore tucked between two cafés.
Warm light spills onto the wet pavement through large glass windows.
She pauses.
Closes her umbrella.
Naturally shakes off the rain.
Opens the wooden glass door.
A small bell rings softly.
The camera follows her inside in one continuous Steadicam shot without cutting.
Inside, shelves are filled with books, warm wooden furniture, soft ambient lighting, and a few customers quietly reading.
The environment feels authentic and lived in.
0:10–0:15 | The Discovery
She slowly walks through the aisles.
Running her fingers gently across book spines.
She notices one beautiful hardcover book displayed alone.
She picks it up.
As she opens it...
A single elegant orange envelope slowly slides out and lands gently on the wooden floor.
The envelope carries a subtle warm glow.
Nothing supernatural.
Just enough to draw attention.
She kneels.
Picks it up carefully.
Opens it.
Inside is a perfectly preserved photograph.
0:15–0:21 | The Photograph
Close-up.
The photograph shows...
Her.
Standing inside this exact bookstore.
Wearing the exact same outfit.
Holding the exact same book.
But...
The photograph is dated 10 years in the future.
The camera slowly pushes toward her face.
She studies every tiny detail.
Her breathing becomes slightly heavier.
No exaggerated acting.
Only subtle confusion and disbelief.
The ambient bookstore sounds become quieter, allowing the emotional moment to breathe.
0:21–0:26 | The Stranger
She slowly raises her head.
Across the bookstore...
A woman is quietly reading beside the window.
The camera slowly reveals her.
She looks almost identical.
Same facial features.
Same gentle smile.
Slightly older.
Elegant cream overcoat.
Hair styled a little differently.
The older woman looks up.
Their eyes meet.
She smiles warmly.
Says nothing.
Everything around them continues normally.
Customers browse books.
A barista prepares coffee.
Rain continues outside.
The world never stops.
0:26–0:30 | The Letter
The older woman calmly closes her book.
Walks toward the exit.
As she passes a nearby wooden table...
She quietly places another identical orange envelope on it.
Without looking back...
She exits through the front door.
The bell rings softly.
The younger woman immediately follows.
She steps outside.
The rainy street is completely normal.
Pedestrians continue walking.
Cars continue passing.
The older woman is nowhere to be seen.
The camera slowly pulls upward as the younger woman looks down at the second envelope in her hands.
Rain continues falling.
Fade to black.
Global Cinematic Direction
Every location must look like a real place in Seoul, filmed on location with practical lighting and premium cinema cameras. Maintain perfect facial consistency for both versions of the protagonist. The older woman should look like the same person approximately ten years later through subtle aging, hairstyle, posture, and wardrobe—not heavy makeup.
Camera movement should remain elegant and restrained: slow dolly shots, gentle Steadicam tracking, natural push-ins, and smooth crane movements. Every transition must have a logical cause-and-effect relationship. No sudden location jumps, no impossible continuity, and no unexplained character movement. Background extras should behave naturally and independently, creating the feeling of a genuine bookstore rather than an AI-generated environment.
The orange motif should appear only in the envelope, its subtle reflections, and the warm bookstore lighting, keeping it tasteful.

Overall tone: quiet, emotional, mysterious, and deeply cinematic, like the opening sequence of a premium Korean mystery film. The mystery comes from the story and performances, not from excessive visual effects, making the entire sequence feel believable and polished.
```
