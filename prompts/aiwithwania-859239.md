Create a cinematic, ultra-realistic 30-second Halloween romance video with a dreamy, mysterious atmosphere.

Scene 1 (0–5 seconds): The Mysterious Room
A young woman with long, dark hair sits quietly in a vintage Halloween-themed room. Antique furniture, warm table lamps, dried autumn leaves, and carved pumpkins decorate the space. She picks up an old, ornate photograph frame containing a portrait of a handsome young man. She gazes at his picture with a shy smile, her eyes reflecting the warm candlelight. The camera slowly moves closer to capture her emotional expression.

Scene 3 (10–15 seconds): A Magical Transformation
She rises and walks toward a mysterious doorway surrounded by glowing amber lights. Her dark outfit and flowing hair move naturally as she steps forward. The doorway opens into a beautifully illuminated Halloween setting, creating a seamless transition into the next scene.

Scene 4 (15–20 seconds): The Unexpected Meeting
She arrives at a cozy outdoor dining area decorated with carved pumpkins, candles, autumn flowers, and vintage lanterns. The young man from the photograph is waiting at a wooden table, wearing a stylish teal sweater and round glasses. She sits across from him, and they exchange warm, slightly nervous smiles.

Scene 5 (20–25 seconds): A Sweet Pumpkin Moment
A small glowing jack-o'-lantern sits between them. The woman gently reaches toward the pumpkin and playfully adjusts its position while the man watches her with an affectionate smile. Their hands briefly meet near the pumpkin, creating a natural, charming romantic moment.

Scene 6 (25–30 seconds): A Fairytale Ending
The camera slowly pulls back, revealing the couple sitting across from each other beneath warm hanging lights, surrounded by pumpkins and autumn decorations. They exchange a happy smile as the candlelight flickers. Finish with a beautiful wide shot of the glowing Halloween setting.

Visual Style: Cinematic realism, warm amber lighting, deep forest-green shadows, detailed textures, shallow depth of field, natural facial expressions, realistic hand movements, subtle camera motion, and atmospheric Halloween lighting.

Character Consistency: Keep the woman's face, hairstyle, clothing, and physical appearance consistent throughout every scene. Maintain the same male character's face and appearance in the photograph and the final date scenes. Ensure realistic transitions and natural movements.

Avoid: Face morphing, inconsistent characters, distorted hands, unnatural expressions, sudden outfit changes, cartoon styling, excessive visual effects, and unrealistic movement.

Mood: Romantic, mysterious, cozy, magical, and cinematic—a Halloween date that feels like a scene from a romantic fantasy film.
